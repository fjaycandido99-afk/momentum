# Era stage art — prompts (8 eras × 4 chapters) + profile backdrop

Gym Arc's four images (`public/era/gym_arc/1-4.jpg`) are the reference. Every
era gets the same treatment: one person, seen from behind or in silhouette,
getting stronger chapter by chapter. The picture follows the DAY, never the
record, so it never goes backwards after a miss.

**Chapters:** 1 Starting (days 1–7) · 2 Building (8–14) · 3 Maintaining
(15–21) · 4 Becoming (22–30).

## Style block — paste this first, every time

> Cinematic black-and-white photograph, nearly monochrome with deep blacks and
> a faint cool tint. One hard light source from above or from a window, dust
> or haze catching the light. A single person seen from BEHIND or in
> silhouette — face never visible. Gritty film grain, shallow depth of field,
> moody, quiet, no text, no logos, no watermark, no lettering anywhere.
> Portrait orientation, 4:5.

**Rules:** the same person and the same place across an era's four images ·
nothing gory, nothing sexualised, no weapons · no text in the picture (the
app sets the words) · don't show screens with readable content.

**Files:** save as `public/era/<key>/1.jpg` … `4.jpg`. Send me whatever size
ChatGPT gives; I'll crop to 720×900 and wire them in.

## Locked In (`locked_in`)
1. **Starting** — A person sitting at a cluttered desk at night, seen from behind, one desk lamp, phone face-down and pushed away, papers everywhere, the first page of a notebook open.
2. **Building** — Same person, same desk, now cleared to one notebook and a laptop, hunched in focus, the lamp pooling light on the work, a cold mug beside them.
3. **Maintaining** — Same person at the same desk before dawn, sleeves rolled up, a wall behind them covered in pinned notes connected by string, absorbed and still.
4. **Becoming** — Same person standing at a tall window at first light, back to camera, the finished work stacked neatly on the desk behind them, calm posture.

## Discipline Era (`discipline`)
1. **Starting** — A person lacing running shoes on a dark doorstep in the rain, seen from behind, a single porch light, reluctant posture.
2. **Building** — Same person running alone down an empty wet street at night under one streetlight, rain in the light.
3. **Maintaining** — Same person on a long bridge in fog at dawn, mid-stride, steady, the city faint behind them.
4. **Becoming** — Same person standing still at the end of the bridge as the first sun breaks through the fog, breath visible, shoulders back.

## Comeback Season (`comeback`)
1. **Starting** — A person sitting on the floor of a dim room against a wall, seen from the side in silhouette, knees up, one shaft of light from a high window touching their feet.
2. **Building** — Same person standing up in the same room, one hand on the window frame, opening the curtain a little, light spilling in.
3. **Maintaining** — Same person walking out through an open doorway into a grey morning, the dark room behind them.
4. **Becoming** — Same person walking up a hill path in open country, back to camera, sun breaking through clouds ahead.

## Stoic Mode (`stoic_mode`)
1. **Starting** — A person seated on rocks at the edge of a stormy sea, seen from behind, waves breaking white around them, hard overcast light.
2. **Building** — Same person standing on the same rocks, feet planted, spray rising around them, posture unmoved.
3. **Maintaining** — Same person sitting cross-legged on the rocks as the storm thins, a single break of light on the water ahead.
4. **Becoming** — Same person standing on the rocks at a calm, mirror-flat sea under soft dawn light, hands loose at their sides.

## Confidence Mode (`confidence`)
1. **Starting** — A person standing in a dark empty theatre wing, seen from behind, peering toward a bright stage they haven't stepped onto.
2. **Building** — Same person taking their first steps onto the stage edge, half in shadow, half in the spotlight.
3. **Maintaining** — Same person standing in the centre of the stage in a single spotlight, back to camera, facing rows of empty seats.
4. **Becoming** — Same person on the stage with arms slightly open, spotlight wide and bright, dust in the beam, the seats now in soft light.

## Study Era (`study`)
1. **Starting** — A person at a long library table at night, seen from behind, a tall stack of unopened books, one green banker's lamp.
2. **Building** — Same person writing in a notebook, books open around them, the lamp light warm on the pages, shelves fading into darkness.
3. **Maintaining** — Same person climbing a rolling ladder between tall library shelves, reaching for a book, light from a high window.
4. **Becoming** — Same person closing a book at the table at dawn, light flooding through tall windows, a neat finished stack beside them.

## 5AM Era (`five_am`)
1. **Starting** — A person sitting on the edge of a bed in a dark room, seen from behind, a phone alarm glowing on the nightstand, window still black.
2. **Building** — Same person standing at a kitchen counter in the dark, a kettle steaming in the light of the open fridge or one small lamp, window deep blue.
3. **Maintaining** — Same person on a balcony or rooftop in the blue hour, coffee in hand, back to camera, the city still asleep below.
4. **Becoming** — Same person on the same rooftop as the sun rises over the skyline, standing tall, light hitting their shoulders.

## Custom era (`custom`) — for eras people name themselves, so nothing specific
1. **Starting** — A person standing at the foot of a long empty staircase in a dark stairwell, seen from behind, one light far above.
2. **Building** — Same person halfway up the staircase, the light above a little stronger.
3. **Maintaining** — Same person near the top, steps worn, haze in the light pouring down.
4. **Becoming** — Same person at the top, stepping out onto an open rooftop into wide morning light.
