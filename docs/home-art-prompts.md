# Home art — 8 images for the Home customization screen

For Francis's "Voxu Home Customization Preview" mockup. Everything else in that
mockup already has art in `public/scenes/` (promise, era, mission, guided,
psychology, laws, wisdom) or `public/achievements/` (relic coins).

**Shared style (paste at the end of every prompt):**
> Cinematic night photography style, deep navy and near-black palette with soft indigo-violet light (#5566F7) and a touch of warm amber where noted. Quiet, premium, minimal. Shallow depth of field, gentle film grain. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no people's faces visible. Square 1:1, subject centered with breathing room so it still reads when cropped to a small rounded tile.

**Export:** 1024×1024 JPG. Save each one to the path shown. The Home rows show them at about 64px and the soundscape tiles at about 80px, so a strong single subject matters more than detail.

## Soundscape tiles

### 2. Waves → `public/scenes/home/sound-waves.jpg`
A single dark ocean wave curling under a night sky, moonlit white spray, deep navy water, indigo glow along the crest.

### 3. Fire → `public/scenes/home/sound-fire.jpg`
A small campfire on a rocky ledge at night, warm amber flames and drifting sparks, dark mountains and a starry indigo sky behind. The only warm-toned image of the set.

### 4. Forest → `public/scenes/home/sound-forest.jpg`
A misty pine forest at night, tall dark trunks, soft indigo moonlight filtering through fog between the trees, a hint of a path.
